ABOUT LES CLEFS D’OR CHINESE TAIPEI

Connecting Taiwan with the World

Les Clefs d’Or Chinese Taipei brings together professional concierges across Taiwan and connects them with the global Les Clefs d’Or network, fostering the exchange of experience, trust and a shared spirit of service.

THE ORGANIZATION

The International Association

Les Clefs d’Or (Golden Keys) is a non-profit international association of professional hotel concierges. Service and Friendship are its guiding principles, supported by membership standards, professional development and a global network that brings concierges together.

CHINESE TAIPEI

The Chinese Taipei Section

The Chinese Taipei section joined the UICH network in 2003. Members serve guests in cities across Taiwan and maintain close professional ties with Les Clefs d’Or colleagues around the world.

OUR PURPOSE

Why We Exist

To help travellers find trusted people, reliable information and practical solutions in an unfamiliar destination.

Advance Professionalism

Continuously learn about destinations, service, the hospitality industry and global travel trends.

Build Trust

Through lasting member relationships, share information, contacts and support so that service can continue across cities.

Represent Taiwan

Present Taiwan’s culture, cities and hospitality to the world through the professionalism of hotel concierge service.

TIMELINE

Milestones in Our International History

1929

Origins in Paris

Hotel concierges in Paris began organising to exchange professional knowledge and experience.

1952

The International Association Takes Shape

The first international congress was held in Cannes, France, helping establish the UICH Les Clefs d’Or network.

2003

Chinese Taipei Joins

Chinese Taipei and Türkiye joined the UICH section network.

2017

Global Membership Exceeds 4,000

The international association reported members across more than 80 countries and 536 destinations.

2025

James Ridenour Becomes International President

Australia’s James Ridenour took office as UICH International President.

2026

70th International Congress · Sydney

The 70th International Congress took place in Sydney in April 2026, with participation from Chinese Taipei.
