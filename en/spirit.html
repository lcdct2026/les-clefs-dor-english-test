MEMBERSHIP

Who Can Become a Member?

Basic Requirements

  • At least five years of front-office experience in a hotel, including at least three years working as a concierge.
  • Two recommendations from association members who have each served for at least one year.

Membership & Role

  • Members must be directly employed by a hotel and work on property.
  • Members generally work at the hotel lobby concierge desk or a related guest-service point.
  • Full members wear the crossed golden keys on their uniform lapel.
Membership Applications in Chinese TaipeiRecruitment periods, document reviews, interviews and application requirements are announced on the association’s official Facebook and Instagram accounts.
