THE GOLDEN KEYS

What Is Les Clefs d’Or?

Les Clefs d’Or is an international association of professional hotel concierges. Wearing the crossed golden keys, members draw on years of hospitality experience, destination knowledge and a trusted global network to help guests turn everyday requests into exceptional experiences.

Professional Concierge

Members meet the qualification and assessment standards of the international association and their member sections, and remain committed to the concierge profession and guest service.

Local Expertise

The value of a concierge goes beyond knowing facts: it comes from understanding a destination firsthand, building trusted relationships and finding thoughtful alternatives.

A Global Network

Members work across countries and destinations, sharing experience, building trust and supporting one another through ongoing connections and annual international congresses.

GLOBAL NETWORK

One Badge, A World of Connections

The international association brings together approximately 4,000 members across more than 80 countries and 530 destinations.
