FROM OUR PRESIDENT

Every encounter should become
a journey to treasure.

The golden keys are more than a badge. They represent professionalism, trust and the bond that connects concierges through service and friendship.

“In Service Through Friendship.”

On behalf of our members across Taiwan, I warmly welcome you to the official website of Les Clefs d’Or Chinese Taipei. I am pleased to share the spirit of hospitality that unites our association and this island.

Taiwan is an extraordinary destination. Vibrant cities, a rich cultural heritage, spectacular landscapes and internationally celebrated cuisine come together to create memorable travel experiences.

As Taiwan continues to welcome travellers from around the world, our members are proud to be ambassadors for both their hotels and Taiwan. Through our knowledge and experience, we hope every visitor can feel the character, warmth and appeal of this island.

The crossed golden keys worn on our lapels symbolise professionalism, trust and a commitment to excellent service.

For a Les Clefs d’Or concierge, service is never just a list of recommendations. It means understanding a guest’s expectations, finding the right answer and connecting them with authentic local people and experiences.

This ability is never the work of one person alone.

Through lasting trust and friendship, members share information, exchange experience and support one another when needed. Whether across cities in Taiwan or among Les Clefs d’Or colleagues around the world, these connections create more possibilities for guests and help us keep learning from one another.

Service Through Friendship is more than a motto to us; it is a commitment grounded in lasting trust.

We hope to create memorable, personalised experiences for every guest and pass on the knowledge, experience and stories accumulated by generations of concierges.

May every journey in Taiwan bring discovery, inspiration and memories to treasure.

Les Clefs d’Or Chinese TaipeiKevin Lin | President
